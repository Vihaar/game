# Card illustrations

`card-atlas.webp` is an original sixteen-cell AI-generated **papercraft** illustration sheet made for The Fold with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-24. It replaces the earlier engraved/painted version at the user's request. The PNG source is retained in the session's generated-image directory; this optimized WebP is the game asset. It is not an output of the optional Cloudflare service. The full visual rules are in `../../ART_DIRECTION.md`.

Prompt: a regular 4×4 grid of sixteen equal square illustrations, no gaps/borders/text/numbers/card frames. Each is a physically handmade layered papercraft miniature viewed from the same elevated three-quarter tabletop angle, consistent soft shadows. Folded, scored, curled/cut matte cardstock; visible paper thickness, fibers, creases, layered edges and tiny imperfections. Strong centered silhouettes with breathing room; nothing crosses cell boundaries. Warm ivory grounds; book-ink navy `#22334B`, coral `#E7774F`, moss teal `#6C9284`, muted violet `#8577A5`, warm paper `#F1E6D2`, tan `#CBB797`. All magic must be literal cut-paper shapes, never glowing energy. No paintings, engraving, glossy plastic/metal or typography. Subjects by row: folded coral ember above paper book, slate-paper shield, teal dew over folded cup, curled violet gust carrying pages; sculptural flare, folded bastion, layered spring well with one leaf, cut-paper firestorm; folded obsidian crystals, curled mist around paper lantern, paper clock with quill hands/no dial numbers, paper thorns around miniature book; folded lightning rune, void through torn layers, folded feather/sword, cardstock book with crescent-moon seal. Coherent physical paper construction across all sixteen cells.

Starter cards use their corresponding cells. Further procedural discoveries deterministically select a cell. When an operator configures the optional server-side Cloudflare integration, newly forged cards can receive a unique cached illustration and semantic title; combat statistics remain deterministic. Unconfigured or unavailable AI does not interrupt play.
