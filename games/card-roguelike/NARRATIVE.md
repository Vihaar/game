# The Fold — a thirst for knowledge

## Promise

You enter the library knowing nothing. You want to learn everything. Each chapter turns an unanswered question into understanding. By the end of the journey, you know everything: the ending delivers that understanding sincerely, without an ironic punishment or a twist that erases what you learned.

The library's bindings have trapped reality in its pages. Learning how its truths fit together lets you unfold the world again. Awe, curiosity and the satisfaction of making connections lead the story. Danger tests your understanding rather than demanding frantic movement.

## Eight chapters

| Library area | Question | Knowledge earned |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intake Hall | How can something be named? | Language turns the unknown into ideas you can use. |
| Mycelium Stacks | How does life connect? | Growth and decay belong to the same living system. |
| Flooded Index | What survives time? | Memory carries the past into every present choice. |
| Glasshouse of Errata | How can things change? | Transformation combines what exists into what could be. |
| Astral Reading Room | What holds the cosmos together? | Small rules connect the smallest detail to the largest whole. |
| Redaction Press | What makes something true? | Understanding requires evidence and the courage to revise a belief. |
| Shattered Archive | Who is doing the learning? | Knowing yourself reveals how your questions shape what you see. |
| Last Binding | How does everything fit together? | All the learned truths unfold into complete understanding. |

The fiction begins at zero understanding and reaches eight connected truths. The eight marks represent this narrative journey; they do not claim the player has enumerated every possible generated recipe.

## Cards and guardians

Cards are discoveries you can hold. Combining cards is learning by connection: a forged card is a new idea, and that idea can become an ingredient again. Guardians personify unanswered questions, doubt, contradiction and incomplete understanding. Existing enemy types may retain their mechanical identities while names and encounter copy express the current chapter's question.

Full permadeath remains: a failed attempt loses its deck, forged cards, resources and chapter progress. The player's recipe journal retains knowledge only and grants no combat power. Three playable starting characters express different approaches to the same thirst for knowledge.

## Pace and presentation

The game leads you along a fixed route. Between encounters, a brief guided transition opens the next illustrated paper stage. Battles are turn-based; no walking, pointer capture, dodge timing or automatic attacks are required. You can stop and read. A Time-price forge clearly previews its immediate enemy action before confirmation. Corruption and Health retain explicit, bounded costs.

The user is comparing three visual directions before choosing the final world look: warm handmade storybook, dark moonlit pop-up book, and colorful paper theater. All three use real generated artwork of physical layered paper. No final visual choice has been assumed.
