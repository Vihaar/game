A THIRST FOR KNOWLEDGE

Eight truths to understand.

    Your turn. Your pace.

    Choose an enemy to target. Cast cards for ink, strike once, and raise your reusable guard once each turn. When you are ready, End turn resolves every living enemy's shown move, refills ink, and draws a fresh hand.

    Guard lasts until enemy resolution. Nothing attacks or refills while you think. There is no walking, aiming, dodge timer or mouse capture.

    Forge: combine any two different owned copies, including forged spells. Check the exact price before confirming. The Time price lets one named enemy perform its shown move immediately. Other modes exchange health or permanent corruption.

    Keys: 1–5 casts · J strikes · K guards · F forge · M journal. All actions also have visible buttons.

    Full permadeath: death loses this run's health, deck, forged spells and knowledge progress. Recipe discoveries remain in this browser. Each cleared binding heals just 1 HP.
