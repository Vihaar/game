YOUR JOURNAL

Notes from the climb.

    Your turn. Your pace.

    Choose an enemy to target. Cast runes for ink, strike once, and raise your reusable guard once each turn. When you are ready, End turn resolves every living enemy's shown move, refills ink, and prepares fresh inscriptions.

    Guard lasts until enemy resolution. Nothing attacks or refills while you think. There is no walking, aiming, dodge timer or mouse capture.

    Forge: combine two distinct rune imprints, including forged inscriptions. Check the exact price before confirming. The Time price lets one named enemy perform its shown move immediately. Other modes exchange health or permanent corruption.

    Keys: 1–5 casts · J strikes · K guards · F forge · M journal. All actions also have visible buttons.

    Full permadeath: death loses this run's health, grimoire, forged spells and knowledge progress. Recipe discoveries remain in this browser. Each cleared binding heals just 1 HP.
