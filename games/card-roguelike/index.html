A little field guide.

Explore: WASD to walk. Click the room for mouse look. Esc releases the mouse. Arrow keys also turn the camera. F opens the forge between battles.

Fight: Movement pauses during an encounter. Click an enemy or press Q/E to choose a target. Click cards or press 1–5 to cast. Spend up to 3 ink each turn, then press Enter. Block expires after the enemies attack.

Forge: Choose two distinct copies from your deck, preview the result, then forge. Both inputs are consumed. Nothing happens if the recipe is invalid.

Win: Clear all three chambers. A new run shuffles enemies, drops, and the draw pile. Run progress resets on reload.
