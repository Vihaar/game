YOUR JOURNAL

Notes from the climb.

What the library remembers

Three casts teach a spell to the library. A guardian on a later floor can use it against you.

Between battles, you may tear out a spell. You lose every owned copy; the library loses its memory of that spell.

The climb

    Your turn. Your pace.

    Choose an enemy to target. Select a spell on the left page, then cast it from the right page for Focus. Strike once, and raise your reusable guard once each turn. When you are ready, End turn resolves every living enemy's shown move, refills Focus, and prepares fresh inscriptions.

    Guard lasts until enemy resolution. Nothing attacks or refills while you think. There is no walking, aiming, dodge timer or mouse capture.

    Combine: select any two reusable imprints, including the same idea twice or a spell you already forged. The ingredients stay in your book. New discoveries are free between fights. In combat, pay 1 Focus and the selected price shown before confirming. Repeated casting teaches your inventions to the library.

    Keys: 1–5 casts · J strikes · K guards · F forge · M journal. All actions also have visible buttons.

    Full permadeath: death loses this run's health, grimoire, forged spells and knowledge progress. Recipe discoveries remain in this browser. Each cleared binding heals just 1 HP.

    Repeated casting teaches the library your spells. Check the journal to see what it remembers. Later guardians announce copied magic before using it. Between battles you can forget a spell, losing every copy and erasing its danger.
