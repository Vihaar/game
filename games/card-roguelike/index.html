Read the room.

Walk: WASD. Click the view for mouse look; Esc releases it. Arrows turn/move without mouse capture. Space is a short dodge, with no invincibility.

Fight: J or left click repeats your basic attack. K or right click raises a brief block. Move out of the marked ground before the enemy's countdown ends. Quills travel after their windup; cover stops them.

Spells: Click a card or press 1–5. T cycles the target. R redraws for 1 ink. Ink returns over time; healing is scarce.

Forge: F opens this panel anywhere. Any two owned copies can combine, including already-forged cards. Read the price, then commit. There is no recipe-depth limit. A channel does not pause enemies, and locks attacking/blocking.

Survive: Enemies show HP, the next move, its timer and damage above their heads. Corruption permanently increases incoming damage for this run. Reach all eight areas and defeat the Unbinder.
